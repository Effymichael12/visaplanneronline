Understanding the Canada Study Permit

A Canada Study Permit is a crucial document issued by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) that allows South African students to study at designated learning institutions (DLIs) in Canada.

  • Designated Learning Institution (DLI)

    You must have an acceptance letter from a DLI to apply for a study permit. We help you verify your institution's status.

  • Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL/TAL)

    Most students need a PAL or TAL from the province or territory they plan to study, confirming their allocation within the national cap.

Your path to studying in Canada, simplified into actionable steps for South African students.

Understanding the Canada Visitor Visa (TRV)

A Temporary Resident Visa (TRV), commonly known as a visitor visa, is an official document issued by a Canadian visa office that is placed in your passport to show that you have met the requirements for admission to Canada as a temporary resident.

It is required for foreign nationals who wish to visit Canada for tourism, to visit family and friends, or for short-term business activities. Our experts ensure your application demonstrates strong ties to your home country and clear intent to return.

Canada Visa FAQ

Answers to what South African applicants ask us most about the Study Permit and Visitor Visa process.

What is a PAL or TAL and when is it required?

A Provincial or Territorial Attestation Letter (PAL/TAL) confirms your place within that province or territory's allocation under Canada's national study permit cap. Most college and undergraduate applicants need one alongside their Letter of Acceptance before they can apply — we'll confirm whether your programme requires it.

What documents and evidence do I need for my Canada visa application?

It depends on the route. Study Permit applicants need a Letter of Acceptance from a DLI, a PAL/TAL where required, proof of funds and a valid passport. Visitor (TRV) applicants need a valid passport, proof of funds, and evidence of their travel purpose and ties to South Africa. We review your full document set before you submit.

Can my parents or a sponsor fund my study permit application?

Yes. IRCC accepts sponsor funding provided you can clearly show the relationship and the sponsor's financial capacity, usually through bank statements, employment letters, or a notarised letter of support. We'll help you organise this evidence in the format IRCC expects.

Do I need to provide biometrics for a Canadian visa?

Most Study Permit and Temporary Resident Visa applicants need to provide biometrics (fingerprints and a photo) at a Visa Application Centre as part of the process. We'll confirm this requirement for your specific application and help you book the appointment.

How do I check current IRCC processing times?

IRCC publishes current processing time estimates on its official website, broken down by application type and country of application. Times change regularly, so we check the latest guidance with you rather than relying on a fixed figure.

What should a letter of explanation cover?

A letter of explanation should clearly set out your purpose for travelling or studying, your ties to South Africa, and your plans after your visit or studies end. It should be honest and specific to your circumstances — we help you structure it so it answers what the visa officer needs to see.

What happens if my study permit or visitor visa is refused?

A refusal letter will state the specific reason IRCC was not satisfied. We'll review it with you, strengthen the evidence or explanation that fell short, and help you prepare a more complete reapplication.
