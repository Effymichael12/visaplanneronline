Understanding the Visa

A Canada Study Permit is a crucial document issued by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) that allows foreign nationals to study at designated learning institutions (DLIs) in Canada.

  • Designated Learning Institution (DLI)

    You must have an acceptance letter from a DLI to apply for a study permit. We help you verify your institution's status.

  • Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL/TAL)

    Most students need a PAL or TAL from the province or territory they plan to study, confirming their allocation within the national cap.

Understanding the Visa

A Temporary Resident Visa (TRV), commonly known as a visitor visa, is an official document issued by a Canadian visa office that is placed in your passport to show that you have met the requirements for admission to Canada as a temporary resident.

It is required for foreign nationals who wish to visit Canada for tourism, to visit family and friends, or for short-term business activities. Our experts ensure your application demonstrates strong ties to your home country and clear intent to return.

The Application Journey

A streamlined path from your initial consultation to boarding your flight.

1. Gather Documents

Compile necessary identification, financial proof, and travel history.

2. IRCC Portal

Submit your finalized application securely via the official portal.

3. Biometrics

Attend your appointment to provide fingerprints and a photograph.

4. Decision

Receive your passport back with the final visa decision.

Preparation Checklist
