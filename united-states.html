US Visa Document Requirements for F-1 Applicants

  • Passport: Valid for travel to the United States.
  • DS-160 Confirmation: Nonimmigrant Visa Application confirmation page.
  • Form I-20: Provided by your SEVP-approved school.
  • SEVIS Info & Fee: Evidence of SEVIS registration and I-901 fee payment.
  • Visa Fee Receipt: Application fee payment receipt.
  • Academic Records: Transcripts, diplomas, degrees, or certificates.
  • Evidence of Academic Preparation: Standardized test scores required by your school.
  • Financial Endorsement: Proof of funds to cover educational, living and travel costs.
  • Circumstantial Information: Evidence of your intent to depart the U.S. after your studies.

Interview Preparation

During your visa interview, the consular officer will determine whether you are qualified to receive a visa. They may ask about various aspects of your application, including:

Academic Background Program Choice Institution Choice Career Plans Funding Plans After Course Overall Circumstances
"The goal is not to memorize a script. The goal is to understand your own application and answer honestly, clearly and naturally."

Important Notice

A visa is not a guarantee of admission to the United States. Admission is determined by U.S. Customs and Border Protection at the port of entry.

B1/B2 Visitor Visa for South Africans

Understanding the B1/B2 Visa

The B1/B2 is the nonimmigrant visa South Africans most often apply for to travel to the United States temporarily. It combines two purposes — business and tourism — into a single, versatile travel document.

  • 💼
    B1 – Business Purposes

    Travel for business activities, including attending scientific, educational, professional, or business conventions, or negotiating contracts.

  • 🌴
    B2 – Tourism & Leisure

    Tourism, vacation, visiting friends or relatives, medical treatment, and participation in social events hosted by fraternal, social, or service organizations.

What it is NOT for

To maintain the integrity of your travel plans, it's crucial to understand the limitations. This visa does not permit:

  • Study
  • Employment
  • Paid performances
  • Work as a crewmember
  • Working on foreign press, radio, film, etc.
  • Permanent residence

USA Visa FAQ

Answers to what South African applicants ask us most about the F-1 and B1/B2 process.

What is the DS-160 and how do I complete it accurately?

The DS-160 is the Online Nonimmigrant Visa Application required for every F-1 and B1/B2 applicant. It asks for your personal, travel, and background details, and the confirmation page is needed for your appointment. We review your DS-160 with you before submission to catch errors that commonly lead to delays or refusals.

What documents do I need for my US visa application?

It depends on your visa category. F-1 applicants need their passport, DS-160 confirmation, Form I-20, SEVIS fee receipt and academic records. B1/B2 applicants need a valid passport, DS-160 confirmation, application fee receipt, and evidence of their travel purpose and ties to South Africa. We review your full document set before your appointment.

How do I book a US visa appointment in South Africa?

Once your DS-160 is submitted and your fee is paid, you book your biometrics and interview appointment through the official U.S. Embassy or Consulate booking system for your region. We'll guide you through this step and help you understand current wait times.

What happens if my US visa application is refused?

A refusal doesn't permanently bar you from reapplying. We'll review the reason given, help you identify what was missing or unclear in your case, and strengthen your documentation and interview preparation before you apply again.

Can my parents or a sponsor fund my F-1 studies?

Yes. Parents, family members, or an organisation can sponsor your studies, provided you can show clear evidence of the relationship and the sponsor's ability to cover your costs. This is typically reflected on your I-20 and supported with bank statements or an affidavit of support.

What evidence supports my plan to return to South Africa?

Consular officers look for ties that show you intend to depart the US after your trip or studies — things like employment, property, family commitments, or enrolment plans back home. We help you organise this evidence clearly for both your application and interview.

How much does a US visa application cost in total?

The government application fee is US$185 for both the F-1 and B1/B2 categories. F-1 applicants also pay a US$350 I-901 SEVIS fee. These are official fees payable to the US government and are separate from any preparation service you choose with us.
