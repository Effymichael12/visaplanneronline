Important Documents

  • Passport: Valid for travel to the United States.
  • DS-160 Confirmation: Nonimmigrant Visa Application confirmation page.
  • Form I-20: Provided by your SEVP-approved school.
  • SEVIS Info & Fee: Evidence of SEVIS registration and I-901 fee payment.
  • Visa Fee Receipt: Application fee payment receipt.
  • Academic Records: Transcripts, diplomas, degrees, or certificates.
  • Evidence of Academic Preparation: Standardized test scores required by your school.
  • Financial Endorsement: Proof of funds to cover educational, living and travel costs.
  • Circumstantial Information: Evidence of your intent to depart the U.S. after your studies.

Important Notice

A visa is not a guarantee of admission to the United States. Admission is determined by U.S. Customs and Border Protection at the port of entry.

B1/B2 Visitor Visa

Understanding the B1/B2 Visa

The B1/B2 is a nonimmigrant visa designed for individuals traveling to the United States temporarily. It expertly combines two distinct purposes into a single, versatile travel document.

  • 💼
    B1 – Business Purposes

    Travel for business activities, including attending scientific, educational, professional, or business conventions, or negotiating contracts.

  • 🧳
    B2 – Tourism & Leisure

    Tourism, vacation, visiting friends or relatives, medical treatment, and participation in social events hosted by fraternal, social, or service organizations.

What it is NOT for

To maintain the integrity of your travel plans, it's crucial to understand the limitations. This visa does not permit:

  • Study
  • Employment
  • Paid performances
  • Work as a crewmember
  • Working on foreign press, radio, film, etc.
  • Permanent residence
