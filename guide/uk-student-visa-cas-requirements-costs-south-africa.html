UK student visa fee & financial checklist (2026)

South African international students applying for a UK student visa must satisfy both UKVI immigration fees and strict 28-day financial holding rules before a CAS reference is generated by their institution.

Visa application fee

£558 (~R12,400). A mandatory upfront cost.

Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS)

£776 per year (~R17,200 per year). Mandatory for every year of study.

VFS biometrics appointment

£0 for a standard appointment. Optional add-ons range from R50 to R800.

Priority processing upgrade

£500 (~R11,200). An optional 5-day decision service.

Understanding the Pre-CAS financial credibility check

UK universities act as licensed student sponsors. Before issuing your CAS, the university admissions team will audit your bank accounts to make sure your liquid funds have stayed uninterrupted for at least 28 consecutive days in an eligible bank (for example Standard Bank, FNB, ABSA or Capitec).
