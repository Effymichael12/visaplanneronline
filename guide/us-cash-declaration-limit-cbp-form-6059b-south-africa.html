How Much Physical Cash Can You Bring into the USA from South Africa Without Fines?

You can bring an unlimited amount of cash into the United States, but if you or your travelling family carry a combined $10,000 USD or more in currency or monetary instruments, you must declare it on CBP Form 6059B to avoid cash seizure, fines of up to $500,000, or imprisonment.

US cash declaration and pre-clearance rules

Navigating US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) inspection points as a South African traveller requires strict compliance with currency reporting laws and modern biometric pre-clearance screening.

Group and family rule

The $10,000 threshold applies to a family or group travelling together on a single customs declaration form, not per individual traveller.

Reportable monetary instruments

Physical cash (USD or ZAR), traveller’s cheques, endorsement-ready cheques and money orders all count toward your $10,000 limit.

CBP Form 6059B

Failing to declare $10,000 or more can result in severe federal penalties, immediate cash confiscation and long-term entry bans.

Biometric and health security updates at airport pre-clearance

Digital facial verification

Pre-clearance hubs (such as Abu Dhabi) now use digital biometric facial comparison rather than manual passport handling to verify travellers before boarding US-bound flights.

Mandatory declarations

Travellers must confirm health-related travel history and declare all agricultural items, including meats, fruits and seeds, to CBP officers before departure.
