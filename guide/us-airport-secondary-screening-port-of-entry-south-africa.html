Key US airport immigration and customs rules

Landing in the United States or passing through international pre-clearance hubs (such as Abu Dhabi or major European transit points) can be an intimidating experience. Being caught unprepared or failing to understand customs regulations can turn a simple holiday or business trip into a stressful interrogation.

Pre-clearance awareness

Immigration checks often occur before boarding your final US-bound flight, locking in your entry status overseas.

Prohibited items and strict fines

Bringing biltong, fresh citrus fruits or traditional bulbs without proper declaration can trigger automatic fines of up to $1,000 under agricultural protection laws.

Consistent interview answers

CBP officers cross-examine your stated travel purpose against your visa class (B1/B2 visitor or F-1 student). Inconsistencies lead directly to secondary holding rooms.

How to prepare before you fly

Know your itinerary, your accommodation and your funding, and make sure your answers match your visa class and supporting documents. Declare all food, plant and agricultural items on your customs form rather than hoping they go unnoticed.
