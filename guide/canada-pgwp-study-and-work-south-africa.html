Can South African Students Work in Canada After Graduation?

Yes. A Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) gives open work authorization from 8 months to 3 years, depending on the length of your completed study program. A qualifying 2-year degree or master’s earns the full 3-year PGWP, and it can only be issued once per person.

Why choose Canada as a South African academic?

Canada is one of the most practical destinations for South Africans who want international higher education combined with post-study career options. Lower average tuition than the US, plus off-campus work rights, gives you a sustainable financial safety net.

Lower tuition costs

Canadian post-secondary tuition rates are generally more competitive than private US universities.

International scholarships

Many Canadian designated learning institutions (DLIs) offer entry-level merit scholarships based on secondary school results.

Off-campus student work

Eligible full-time international students can work off-campus during academic semesters to help offset daily living expenses.

Navigating Canada’s Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)

Permit duration

An eligible program of 8 months to 2 years earns a PGWP matching your program length. A qualifying 2-year degree or master’s program earns a full 3-year PGWP.

Transition period

Once you receive your final academic transcripts, off-campus student work rights end immediately. Apply for your PGWP promptly to keep your status.

Once-in-a-lifetime rule

The PGWP is issued once per person. You cannot get a second PGWP, even if you complete another Canadian degree later.
