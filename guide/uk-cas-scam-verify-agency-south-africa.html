Red flags of a fake UK CAS offer

Fraudsters target South African students with fake university portals and urgent messages. Check every one of these before you send money or documents.

Guaranteed CAS

No organization or university can 100% guarantee that the government will issue your CAS. Any such promise is a warning sign.

Fake university portals

Look-alike websites and unofficial email addresses are used to harvest fees and personal details. Verify the portal independently.

Upfront deposits

Demands for deposits through unofficial channels are a classic scam tactic. Never pay into an account you have not verified.

Fake “discounts”

Offers to cut official fees and high-pressure emails push you to act before you think. Official fees are fixed.

Why verification matters now

UK student visa scams have risen by 38%, and applicants who lose money or hand over documents to fraudsters can see their studies delayed or cancelled. Checking suspicious emails, chats and your agent’s background before you commit protects both your savings and your application.
