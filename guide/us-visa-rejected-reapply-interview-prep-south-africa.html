Why US visas get refused & what to fix before you reapply

Most refusals for South African applicants come down to a short list of causes. Knowing which one applies to you is the difference between a stronger second attempt and a repeat refusal.

No mandatory waiting period

The US sets no required wait after a rejection, so you can reapply immediately once your file is genuinely stronger.

INA Section 214(b)

The usual reason for visitor and student refusals: weak ties to South Africa or insufficient proof you will return.

Missing documentation

Gaps in financial, employment or travel evidence leave the officer unable to approve. Fix these before you rebook.

Know the exact reason

Guessing your way through the next application is a recipe for failure. Pinpoint why the consular officer turned you down first.

Rebuilding your application the right way

Having been through the US system myself, I know how critical it is to understand your refusal before you pay a new fee. A focused review of your refusal details, corrected evidence and live interview practice give you the best chance at the Johannesburg (Sandton) or Cape Town consulate.
