UK increases student visa living cost requirements by 2.7% for South Africans | Visa Planner Online

Planning a move to the UK for your studies? There is a crucial update to factor into your financial preparations right now.

Starting 30 November 2026, the UK government is raising its financial maintenance requirements by 2.7% for international students. If you are preparing your UK student visa application from South Africa, meeting the updated numbers down to the last rand is non-negotiable.

The new UK student living costs

Depending on where your university is located, the maintenance funds you must prove have shifted (for a 9-month period):

  1. Studying in London: approximately £14,130 (roughly R309,000).
  2. Studying outside London: £10,827 (roughly R237,500).

These figures are separate from your first-year university tuition fees. For a complete fee breakdown, see our UK visa guide for South Africans.

Beware the 28-day rule trap

Having the money in your account is only half the battle. UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) enforces a rigid timeline:

  1. Your funds must sit in an eligible bank account for at least 28 consecutive days.
  2. The final day of that 28-day period must fall within 31 days of the date you submit your online visa application.
  3. If your balance dips below the required amount for even a single day in that window, your visa faces refusal.

Parental sponsorship requirements

If your parents or guardians are funding your education, you cannot simply attach their bank statements. South African applicants must also provide:

  1. An official, unabridged birth certificate linking you directly to your sponsors.
  2. A signed, formal letter of consent from your parents approving the use of their funds for your UK living expenses.

Financial and bank statement technicalities are a leading cause of avoidable student visa rejections. Don’t risk your timeline on a guess.
