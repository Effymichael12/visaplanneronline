Updated 2026 US visa fees in South Africa

The government fees below are what you pay before the interview. Rand amounts are estimates and move with the exchange rate on the day you pay.

DS-160 form

Free to complete online.

MRV interview fee

$185 USD (about R3,000).

Visa Integrity Fee

$250 USD (about R4,200) for designated categories.

Total government fees

$435 USD, about R7,200 per applicant for designated categories.

Budget beyond the fees: proof of funds

The fees are only part of the real cost. Consular officers also check that you can afford the trip or studies without sudden, unverified deposits.

US B1/B2 visitor visa financial benchmarks

Consular officers at the US Embassy in Johannesburg and the Consulate in Cape Town check whether your declared trip costs match your bank statements. They are looking for steady, explainable money, not sudden lump-sum deposits (a “bank seasoning” red flag).

Savings ratio

Your total trip budget should be a reasonable fraction of your liquid savings. If your trip costs R30,000, your bank statement should comfortably show more than that.

Consistent income

Provide 3 to 6 months of stamped bank statements showing steady salary or business earnings, not large deposits made right before your appointment.

US F1 student visa financial requirements

For parents and sponsors, the Form I-20 sets the standard you are measured against.

Form I-20 reference

The Form I-20 issued by the US institution sets your minimum financial requirement, combining tuition, living expenses and health insurance.

Education loans

If you are using a financial institution loan, present the official, signed loan approval letter at the embassy window.

Sponsor documentation

Parents acting as financial sponsors must provide official salary slips, employment confirmation letters and SARS tax returns.
