How to Fix a Mistake on Your DS-160 US Visa Application: A Step-by-Step Guide for South Africans | Visa Planner Online

By Effy | Visa Planner Online

When you’re filling out a DS-160 form for a US visitor or student visa, it is completely normal to feel a bit overwhelmed. I’ve been through the US system myself—from managing my own F-1 and OPT to helping friends navigate American bureaucracy—and I see applicants make typing errors or selection mistakes on their forms all the time.

If you hit submit on your DS-160 and immediately realised you made a mistake, don’t panic and pay a brand-new fee right away. Here is exactly how you handle it.

Step 1: Check if you actually submitted the form

The DS-160 system is forgiving if you haven’t clicked final submission yet. You can save your application progress, exit, and come back using your Application ID to make edits whenever you need.

Step 2: What to do if you already submitted

If you’ve already signed and submitted the form with an error, you cannot edit that specific submitted document. Instead, you need to create a corrected version:

  1. Go back to the CEAC DS-160 website.
  2. Select your location and click “Retrieve an Application”.
  3. Enter your previous application’s barcode number.
  4. Once loaded, click the option to “Create a New Application”.

This copies over most of your previous data so you don’t have to start from scratch, letting you fix the error and submit a brand-new form with a new barcode.

Step 3: Update your interview scheduling profile

Once you have your new DS-160 barcode, you need to update your appointment profile on the official scheduling portal. If the system doesn’t let you swap the barcode online before your interview in Johannesburg or Cape Town, contact customer support immediately and bring both confirmation pages to your appointment.
